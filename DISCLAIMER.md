# Defensive-use disclaimer

This repository contains defensive threat-hunting and detection content. Use it only in systems and networks you own or are explicitly authorized to monitor.

The queries and rules are hypotheses, not proof of compromise. They may produce false positives, omit activity, or behave differently when telemetry is incomplete. Test in a non-production workspace, assess privacy and retention requirements, document local changes, and use your organization's incident-response process before containment.
